# Vehicle: Tesla Model 3 / Model Y
# Section: SI-20-44-001 / WSM 4401
# System: High Voltage Powertrain & Safety
# Years: 2020-2026

## HIGH VOLTAGE PYROFUSE DISCONNECT & DE-ENERGIZATION PROTOCOL (DTC BMS_a064 / BMS_u018)

### 1. High Voltage Isolation Procedure
- High-Voltage Interlock Loop (HVIL): The HVIL loop is a continuous 12V circuit that loops through every high-voltage connector. If any connector is unseated, the BMS contactors instantly open.
- Low-Voltage 12V / 16V Battery Disconnect: Always disconnect low-voltage first to de-energize the contactor coils before servicing the penthouse.
- First Responder Cut Loop: Cut loop is located under the front hood on the passenger cowl. Do not touch or cut orange HV cabling.
- Verification of Zero Energy State: Connect CAT-IV 1000V rated digital multimeter to high-voltage bus terminals. Verify residual voltage is under 12V DC before proceeding with any service.

### 2. Torque Specifications
- High Voltage Penthouse Cover Fasteners: 9 Nm (80 lb-in)
- High Voltage Busbar Retaining Bolts: 18 Nm (13.3 ft-lb)
- Pyro-fuse Retaining Fasteners: 8 Nm (71 lb-in)
- Inverter Ground Lug Fasteners: 14 Nm (10.3 ft-lb)

### 3. Safety Warnings
DANGER: Lethal high voltage (350V - 450V DC) present in traction pack. Service only with Class 0 (1,000V rated) electrical insulating gloves inspected within last 6 months. Never bypass the HVIL circuit.
