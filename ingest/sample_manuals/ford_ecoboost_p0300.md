# Vehicle: Ford F-150 EcoBoost 3.5L
# Section: TSB 21-2119 / WSM 303-07A
# System: Engine Powertrain
# Years: 2018-2024

## IGNITION SYSTEM & RANDOM MISFIRE DIAGNOSTIC PROCEDURE (DTC P0300 / P0302)

### 1. Diagnostic Verification
- Verify spark plug electrode gap: Factory specification is strictly 0.030 in (0.76 mm). Over-gapped plugs caused by spark erosion under twin-turbo boost pressures exceeding 14 psi will blow out spark kernel, triggering intermittent high-load misfires.
- Direct-Injection Carbon Build-up: Inspect intake valves using borescope. Direct-injected engines lack port fuel washing; heavy carbon buildup on intake valve tulips causes air tumbling turbulence and cold-start misfire codes.
- VCT Camshaft Phasers (TSB 21-2119): Check for cold-start rattle lasting 2-5 seconds. Worn locking pins in intake camshaft phasers cause timing deviation and intermittent P0016/P0300.
- Fuel Rail Pressure Sensor (FRP): Verify low-pressure fuel pump delivery (55-65 psi) and high-pressure rail pressure (2,150 psi at idle, up to 2,900 psi under wide-open throttle).

### 2. Torque Specifications
- Spark Plugs: 15 Nm (133 lb-in)
- Ignition Coil Retaining Bolts: 10 Nm (89 lb-in)
- Intake Manifold Upper Bolts: 10 Nm (89 lb-in) in cross pattern
- High-Pressure Fuel Pump Mounting Bolts: 12 Nm + 45 deg

### 3. Safety Warnings
WARNING: Fuel system remains under extreme pressure up to 3,000 psi. Always relieve high-pressure fuel system using scan tool routine before opening fuel lines. Wear wrap-around eye protection.
